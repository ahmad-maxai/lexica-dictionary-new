# LEXICA — Assignment 018: Reviewer Recruitment and Qualification Framework

**Branch:** `feature/assignment-018-fulfulde-human-validation`
**Scope:** Practical recruitment process and qualification standards for fluent Fulfulde speakers.

---

## 1. Recruitment Priority & Channels

To ensure authentic linguistic feedback on the 20-entry Fulfulde Recovery Corpus, recruitment prioritizes:
1. **Fluent Nigerian Adamawa Fulfulde speakers** (Adamawa, Taraba, Gombe, Borno, Bauchi).
2. **Other Nigerian Fulfulde speakers** with clearly identified regional dialects (Sokoto, Zaria, Kano).
3. **Fluent Pulaar or Pular speakers** for entries belonging to those specific western varieties.
4. **Professional linguists** specializing in Atlantic-Congo languages and Fulfulde lexicography.

### Recruitment Notice Template
> **Call for Fulfulde Lexicography Reviewers — LEXICA Multilingual Project**
> We are seeking fluent speakers of Fulfulde (especially Nigerian Adamawa, Sokoto, Pulaar, or Pular varieties) and linguistic researchers to evaluate a prototype 20-entry digital dictionary corpus. Your feedback will ensure natural phrasing, accurate regional spelling, and authentic example sentences. Participation is voluntary, anonymized, and governed by strict privacy standards. To participate, please complete the review form or contact the project maintainers.

---

## 2. Reviewer Qualification Fields

When onboarding participants, the system captures:
- Reviewer ID (pseudonymous, e.g., `FF-REV-001`).
- Self-described language proficiency.
- Native or non-native speaker status.
- Region or community (voluntarily provided).
- Dialect or variety.
- Years of speaking experience (voluntarily provided).
- Literacy in Latin Fulfulde orthography.
- Familiarity with Adlam script (`𞤀𞤣𞤤𞤢𞤥`).
- Review date and consent confirmation.
