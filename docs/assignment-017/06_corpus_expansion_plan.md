# LEXICA — Assignment 017: Corpus Expansion Plan

**Branch:** `feature/assignment-017-fulfulde-validation`
**Scope:** Defensible roadmap for scaling the Fulfulde native corpus from 20 to 1,000+ entries.

---

## 1. Expansion Targets

- **Phase 1 (Immediate Target): 100 Verified Entries**
  - Focus: High-frequency Adamawa pastoral, family, and survival vocabulary.
- **Phase 2 (Secondary Target): 250 Verified Entries**
  - Focus: Environment, flora/fauna, weather, and trade terms.
- **Phase 3 (Long-Term Target): 1,000+ Entries**
  - Focus: Comprehensive regional lexicon across Adamawa, Sokoto, and Gombe dialects.

---

## 2. Priority Vocabulary Domains

1. **Family and Relationships:** Kinship terms, marriage, naming ceremonies.
2. **Food and Agriculture:** Milk processing (*kosam*, *kiri*), grains (*maaro*, *gero*), pastoral diet.
3. **Animals and Environment:** Cattle terminology, transhumance routes, weather patterns.
4. **Health and Body:** Traditional anatomy, healing, hygiene.
5. **Education and Learning:** Traditional wisdom (*gandal*), Quranic/Ajami transmission.
6. **Movement and Travel:** Pastoral migration, navigation, distance.
7. **Trade and Everyday Activities:** Market commerce, craftwork, pastoral tools.
8. **Culture and Social Life:** *Pulaaku* ethics, greetings, communal storytelling.

---

## 3. Strict Acceptance Criteria for New Entries

A new entry **MUST NOT** be admitted to the production core pack unless it satisfies:
1. Genuine native Fulfulde definition explaining the concept in Fulfulde (no English-only glosses).
2. Verified orthography adhering to Bamako/NERDC standards (`ɓ`, `ɗ`, `ƴ`, `ŋ`, `ɲ`).
3. Authentic example sentence with English translation.
4. Explicit regional variety label (`nigerian_adamawa`, etc.).
5. Passing automated validation and SQLite integrity checks.
