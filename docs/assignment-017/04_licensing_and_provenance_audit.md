**Scope:** Audit of legal declarations, attribution requirements, and provenance for the recovery corpus.

---

## 1. Licensing Analysis

1. **CC0 1.0 Declaration:** Applies strictly to project-authored original recovery corpus entries authored directly for the LEXICA Multilingual Lexicography Project.
2. **Lexica Project License:** Defined as an internal permissive attribution framework ensuring free non-commercial and commercial educational reuse with proper credit. It does not restrict or override upstream CC0 terms.
3. **Attribution Requirements:** All recovery entries cite their origin (`Lexica Curated Adamawa Corpus` or `Tatoeba fuc Corpus`) and corresponding license (`CC0 1.0` or `CC BY 2.0 FR`).
4. **Restricted Materials Excluded:** BUK CSNL, SIL Webonary, and copyrighted print dictionaries remain excluded to respect intellectual property rights.
