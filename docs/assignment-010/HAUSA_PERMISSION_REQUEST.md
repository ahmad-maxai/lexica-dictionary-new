**Subject:** Permission Request: Incorporating Ƙamusun Hausa into the LEXICA Offline Multilingual Dictionary App  

Dear Dr. Shamsuddeen Muhammad, HausaNLP Team, and Leadership of the Centre for Research in Nigerian Languages, Translation, and Folklore (CRNLT&F / CSNL), Bayero University, Kano,

Warm greetings from the LEXICA Dictionary Project.

We are developing **LEXICA**, an offline-first multilingual educational dictionary application designed specifically to serve speakers, students, and learners on low-memory Android devices (including Android Go smartphones) where reliable internet connectivity is limited or unavailable.

During our Assignment 010 lexicography quality audit, we inspected `https://kamusunhausa.hausanlp.org/` and recognize **Ƙamusun Hausa**—originally compiled by CSNL (Bayero University, Kano) and digitized by HausaNLP—as the premier authoritative **Hausa-to-Hausa monolingual dictionary** available online. Because we refuse to scrape restricted websites, bypass access controls, or redistribute copyrighted lexical data without explicit authorization, we are writing formally to request permission and licensing guidance before incorporating any Ƙamusun Hausa content into LEXICA.

Specifically, we kindly ask for written clarification and permission regarding the following five points:

1. **Authorized Access / Dataset Availability:**
   Whether a legitimate downloadable export, structured JSON/CSV dataset, or authorized API dump of Ƙamusun Hausa can be provided or licensed to our team, rather than relying on the web interface.
2. **Offline Redistribution & Mobile Packaging:**
   Whether we may redistribute the lexical entries inside downloadable, offline-capable SQLite dictionary packs distributed to LEXICA mobile users (primarily Android users across Nigeria and West Africa).
3. **Non-Commercial and Commercial Scope:**
   Whether permission applies solely to non-commercial / free educational distribution (which is our primary priority for learners), and what separate licensing or partnership terms would apply if LEXICA ever introduces paid tiers, institutional licensing, or commercial app store distribution.
4. **Data Modification & SQLite Compilation:**
   Whether technical transformation is permitted—specifically indexing normalized headwords (including hooked consonants `ɓ, ɗ, ƙ, ƴ` and tonal diacritics), normalizing formatting into structured SQLite tables (`Core` and `Extended` layers), separating definition types, and compressing the compiled SQLite database with GZIP—while preserving the fidelity of CSNL/HausaNLP's Hausa definitions without machine alteration.
5. **Attribution, Ownership & Third-Party Exclusions:**
   - Confirmation of the exact copyright holder(s) (e.g., CRNLT&F / CSNL Bayero University, Kano and HausaNLP) and the exact attribution text, citation, and links you require us to display inside the app's entry cards and About/Credits screen.
   - Confirmation that all entries in the authorized export are cleared for this grant and whether any subsets or third-party contributions must be excluded.

We would be honoured to credit **CRNLT&F (CSNL), Bayero University, Kano** and **HausaNLP** prominently in the application UI, metadata, and academic documentation, and to collaborate on community feedback or error reporting channels back to your team.

Thank you very much for your pioneering work in preserving and digitizing Hausa lexicography. We look forward to your guidance and response.
